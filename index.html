    How to load a game you own

    1. On your computer, locate your own PS2 game image file. The emulator may support formats such as ISO; check the Play! version's supported formats.
    2. Scroll to the Play! player below and click inside it.
    3. Use the emulator's own Open or Load file option and choose your game image from your computer.
    4. Wait for the game to load. Keep the file on your computer; this website does not upload or store it.

    For a physical disc, your computer needs a compatible disc drive and the emulator must support reading it. Otherwise, use a game image you are legally allowed to use.
